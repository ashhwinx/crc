For the Railcar Leasing section, I would like the equipment catalog organized primarily by
railcar type, with more specific sizes and configurations available within each category.
The seven main categories should be Boxcars, Covered Hoppers, Open-Top Hoppers, Gondolas,
Centerbeam Flatcars, General-Purpose/Bulkhead Flatcars, and Tank Cars. We do not plan to
feature intermodal equipment or autoracks initially.
Within each category, customers should be able to browse common lengths, cubic capacities,
typical commodities, and loading/unloading configurations. I would like the structure to support
more detailed manufacturer-specific railcar models and technical specification sheets in the
future.
For now, the catalog should function as an equipment sourcing and leasing inquiry tool, rather
than displaying unverified inventory as immediately available.
Under each equipment type, I think it would be best to do a broad outline of the car sizes and
capacities, and some generic info on what industries they serve. Here are some examples:
1. Boxcars - 50-ft standard, 50-ft high-cube, 60-ft standard, 60-ft high-cube, insulated, refrigerated
2. Covered Hoppers - 3,230 cu ft pressure differential, 3,281 cu ft cement/mineral, 4,359 cu ft industrial, 4,650–4,750 cu ft,
5,161–5,400 cu ft grain, 5,660–5,850 cu ft pneumatic, 6,241–6,541 cu ft large-capacity
3. Open-Top Hoppers - 2,400 cu ft, 2,480 cu ft rapid discharge, 2,900 cu ft aggregate, 3,000–4,000 cu ft bulk
4. Gondolas - 42-ft coil car, 52-ft mill gondola, 65/66-ft mill gondola, high-sided gondola, rotary gondola
5. Centerbeam Flatcars - 65-ft, 73-ft, riser-deck, riserless-deck
6. General-Purpose & Bulkhead Flatcars - 50-ft bulkhead, 60-ft bulkhead, 62-ft bulkhead, 60-ft general-purpose, 89-ft specialty
flatcar, heavy-duty flatcar
7. Tank Cars - 13,000–19,000 gallon, 20,000–25,000 gallon, 25,000–30,000+ gallon; insulated, non-insulated, pressure and
non-pressure configurations
