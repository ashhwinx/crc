Harshit, these notes cover how the site looks and reads. Placeholder copy, contact details,
legal pages, and the backend are being handled separately, so they're not covered here.

two new feature ideas.

(section 1) and the value cards (section 6). Cards stay everywhere else.

line (section 6).
3. Rework the hero badges as a spec strip, or remove them (section 4).
4. Make boxcar sizes selectable and carry the choice straight into the cost estimator.
The layout stays as it is (section 3).
5. Connect the estimator to the rest of the site, so every selection and inquiry carries
straight into the cost tool (section 7).
6. Idea for later, not part of this build: a 3D railcar viewer.
7. New FAQ entry for shippers who don't have a rail siding yet.

Use my sketch as the layout. Keep the current text, but tighten each value around its
numbers and why they matter. No card box.
Layout (top to bottom, centered)
1. A large icon as the lead: about 64–80 px, brand red line icon, no box or pill behind it.
2. A short title, one or two words (for example "Environment").
3. Text: the existing description, cut to two or three lines. Lead with why it matters to the
shipper.
4. A stat line: two or three figures separated by thin dividers, set larger and bolder than
the text so they're the first thing read after the title.
Remove the colored top borders, the colored subheads (blue, green, purple), and the
checkmark bullet lists. The stat line replaces the bullets.
Example: 05 Environment
Text: Moving freight from highway to rail cuts fuel use and emissions on every load,
which helps shippers meet their sustainability targets.
Stats: ~500 miles per ton per gallon | Up to 75% fewer emissions than trucks | 3–4
trucks off the road per car

Two columns per row: a small label on the left (date, number, or icon) and a large title
with one or two lines of body text on the right.
Thin dashed rules between rows instead of borders around each item.
A big type contrast: the titles are large, and the body text is small and muted.
No boxes, fills, or shadows. The rows sit directly on the page background.
A subtle scroll effect: rows brighten as they reach the middle of the screen and dim as
they leave.
Text rows instead of cards (flagged sections only)
I want this layout in the two places I flagged, not across the whole site. Cards still work for
the equipment catalog, the estimator, and the other sections.
Where to use it:
Design rules for every section:
One accent color (the brand red) plus neutrals. No blue, green, purple, or orange
accents.
The same icon treatment everywhere: line icons, large, in the accent color, with no
tinted box behind them.
Tighter corners (3–4 px) on what stays boxed: buttons, inputs, and the estimator
panels.

New FAQ entry: shippers without a rail siding
A lot of first-time rail shippers will reach the site without track of their own. Add an FAQ
entry for them, possibly with a short callout near the quote form:
"I don't have a rail siding. Can I still use railcars?" Yes. Three common ways in:
Transload: ship through a nearby transload facility, where cargo moves between truck
and railcar.
Team track: load at a railroad-owned public track near you.
Your own track: reactivate dormant track on your property, or add a new spur. We can
help you find rail-compatible sites, too.
End the entry with a "Tell us your location" button that opens the request form.
